# # ZURI: Fictional Friends, Real Conversations
ZURI is an innovative, AI-powered conversational interface designed to provide empathetic and personalized interactions. This mobile/web application leverages natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning algorithms to understand and respond to users' emotional needs, fostering a sense of companionship and community.
ZURI is built to help people with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) and Social Anxiety Disorder (SAD) practise social skills through interactive role-playing scenarios, in a safe, low-pressure space.
Disclaimer: ZURI is a practice and support tool. It is not a medical device and does not replace professional therapy or mental health care.
Why ZURI?
Mental health conditions like ASD and SAD can make social interaction difficult. Traditional therapy can be costly, geographically limited, and sometimes stigmatized, which stops many people from seeking help.
ZURI offers an accessible alternative: a virtual space where users can rehearse conversations, get real-time feedback, and build confidence at their own pace.
Features
Role-play scenarios: practise everyday situations, from ordering food at a restaurant to attending a job interview.
AI-driven chatbots: characters that respond naturally and empathetically to the user.
Personalised interactions: NLP-driven conversations that adapt to each user.
Real-time feedback: guidance during and after each conversation to build confidence.
Adaptive learning: the experience improves through feedback loops.
Safe and judgement-free: a private space to make mistakes and try again.
How It Works
The user picks a scenario (for example, a job interview or a casual chat).
An AI chatbot plays the other person in the conversation.
The user responds by typing, and the NLP model interprets the message and generates a reply.
The user receives feedback and can retry the scenario to improve.

Ethics, Privacy, and Safety
Because ZURI deals with sensitive conversations, these principles guide the project:
User privacy: conversations should be handled securely, with data protection standards such as GDPR and HIPAA in mind for any future deployment.
Safety: the chatbot should stay supportive and should not give medical advice or replace professional help.
Transparency: users should always know they are talking to an AI.
